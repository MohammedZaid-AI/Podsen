Podsen

Listen to what's worth it.

Tell Podsen how much time you have, and it picks which episodes from your podcasts are worth it.

Install the Podsen app

Works with Spotify, YouTube, Apple Podcasts and Amazon Music.

How it works

  1. Add the shows you already follow. Search for them, or tick a few from what's trending in India, the US or the UK.
  2. Say how much time you have. 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or any number you type.
  3. Get picks that fit. The newest episodes from your shows, one per show where it can, never more than your time. The rest are listed as safe to skip.
  4. Press play. Listen right on the page, or open the episode in Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube or Amazon Music.

Made for a full queue

You follow a lot of shows, there are dozens of episodes you haven't heard, and you're not unsubscribing from any of them. Podsen doesn't ask you to. It tells you what to play in the time you have right now.

  • Picks come only from the shows you add.
  • It never connects to your Spotify or Apple account. It reads each show's public feed.
  • Mark an episode as heard and it stops coming up.
  • Sign in with Google. Delete your account and everything in it whenever you like.

Install Podsen on your phone

No app store needed. Install it once and Podsen opens from its own icon, full screen, like any other app.

iPhone

  1. Open the Podsen app page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Tap Install the Podsen app at the top of this page, then Install.
  2. No install dialog? Tap Chrome's ⋮ menu, top right.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then tap Install.

Sign in with Google once and you stay signed in for 30 days.
